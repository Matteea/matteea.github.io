What RepoReel is

RepoReel is a personal tool, not a product or a service. It runs on one person's computer and publishes short explainer videos to that person's own social accounts. It is not offered to anyone else, there is nothing to buy or subscribe to, and no account can be created.

If you are watching the videos

The videos are published on YouTube, TikTok and Instagram, and watching them is governed by those platforms' terms, not by these. These terms exist to describe what the tool does, for the platforms whose APIs it uses.

The videos describe other people's work

  • Every video is about a publicly available open-source project, and is an independent explainer. It is not official content from that project, is not endorsed by its authors, and never claims to be.
  • Every video credits the project's authors and links its repository.
  • Projects are covered only where their licence permits it, and material used under a licence requiring attribution is credited.
  • Logos and brand marks belong to their owners and appear only to identify the software being discussed.

AI-generated content

The narration is synthesised speech using a clone of the operator's own recorded voice, and the visuals are generated. Every video is labelled with each platform's AI-generated content disclosure, and the caption states it in words. No video imitates another person's voice or likeness.

Accuracy, and getting it wrong

Each video explains one idea from a project in about forty seconds. A human reads and approves the script before any video is made and approves the finished cut before it is published. Even so, a forty-second explanation is a simplification and may be wrong or out of date. Nothing in a video is advice, and the authoritative source for any project is always its own documentation.

If a video misrepresents a project, or if you are a project's author and would rather it did not exist, write to the address below and it will be taken down. No justification is needed.

Platform access

Where a social platform grants RepoReel access to an account, that access is used only to upload videos to that same account and to comment on those videos. It is not used for anything else, and never on an account other than the operator's own. Access can be revoked at any time from the platform's own settings.

The tool is provided as is, for its operator's own use, with no warranty of any kind.
